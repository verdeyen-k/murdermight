How to play

  • The train has 7 cars: the locomotive, rooms 1-10, 11-20, 21-30, 31-40, 41-50 and the caboose.
  • One person is secretly the murderer. Everyone else is innocent.
  • Innocents: do your 5 tasks. When all 5 are done, go to room 25, the meeting room, and accuse someone. Right? You win! Wrong? The person you accused is out, and so are you!
  • Murderer: only you can hold weapons. You start with a 🔪 dagger. After 2 kills you get the 🔫 shotgun (it's loud!). After 4 kills you get the 📡 tracker and can see everyone on the map. Kill all the innocents to win. But anyone who sees your weapon out knows it's you!
  • Hiding: wardrobes and lockers are all over the train. Click it (or press H) to hide inside. Nobody can see you, and you're not on the tracker... but if the murderer stabs your hiding place, you're done!
  • Keys: WASD to walk, mouse to look. E (hold) do a task. F accuse (in room 25). Murderer: click to attack (your weapon comes out for a moment), Q to switch weapons or hold one out.
